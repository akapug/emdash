`@emdash-cms/cloudflare/auth-mail` is the auth-mail mediator: EmDash's own sign-in mail (magic links, sign-up verification, invites, recovery links) issued and sent by a Worker of its own (`@emdash-cms/cloudflare/auth-mail/worker`, RPC only), apart from the site's. Its four operations take fixed requests (a request carrying a subject, a body, a link, a token or a recipient is refused), rate-limit by IP and by recipient, check eligibility, authenticate the privileged ones themselves from the original credential (an `admin`-scoped API or OAuth token, or the admin's session), issue the token and render the message with `@emdash-cms/auth`'s own code, take the mail budget's system lane with the mediator's own token (mail-budget protocol 2), send through its own `EMAIL_SYSTEM` binding, and give the place back and delete the token when the budget or the binding refuses. Answers never carry a token, a link or an address. `status()` answers its marker (`AUTH_MAIL_MARKER`) and the budget's report of its token's tenant and lane. The header checks the provider and the mediator share are in `email-headers`, and the mail-budget protocol number in `mail-budget-protocol`.
